Forecast results

 

1. Where to look at the results

Once a forecast has run, its results are saved with the scenario and can be reviewed with these tools:

Tool

Where

What it shows

Timeline

Main window, "Show Timeline"

The status of every node and well at every time step, colour-coded: bottlenecks, shut-ins, choked-back wells. See Forecast results: Timeline.

Network view

Main window, "Show Network"

The network diagram with the results at each node. See Network view.

Charts

"Forecast Results" ribbon group

Rates, cumulatives, ratios, pressures, shortfalls, economics and GHG at any node, for one or several scenarios; probabilistic P10 / P50 / P90; opportunity gains. See Forecast results: Charts.

Maps

"Forecast Results" ribbon group

Six maps of the patterns (or well bubbles) coloured by a result, at a chosen date. See Forecast results: Patterns.

Export to Excel

"Forecast Results" ribbon group

An .xlsx workbook of the forecast, one sheet per scenario. See Export to Excel.

Dashboard

"Forecast Results" ribbon group

A saved page of charts, text and images, refreshed for any scenario (section 2).

Generate report

"Forecast Results" ribbon group

A Word document built from a saved dashboard (section 3).

Material Balance dashboard

"Forecast Results" ribbon group, when the scenario's "Decline" is "Material Balance"

History match of the material-balance runs. See Material Balance, section 10.

Gain per opportunity

"Forecast" ribbon group

The production each opportunity adds to the scenario (section 4).

On some installations, "Funnel" also appears in the "Forecast Results" group: it opens the Opportunity Funnel, a board of the asset's opportunities across their maturation phases (drag a card to change its phase; double-click it to open the opportunity).

Tip: in every results window, a double right-click on a chart copies it to the clipboard, ready to paste into Word, PowerPoint or an e-mail.

2. Dashboards

A dashboard is a saved page of tiles (charts, text and images) for the asset. It is saved once and redrawn for whichever project and scenario you pick at the top of the page, so the same page can be used for every new forecast.

Click "Dashboard" in the "Forecast Results" group. It opens the results window in dashboard mode and loads your default dashboard. If the asset has no dashboard yet, Precept offers to open the results in dashboard mode so you can create the first one.

2.1 Create a dashboard

1.Click "Create new" and type the "Dashboard name:".

2.Click "Add elements" and choose "Chart (configure in the builder)", "Text" or "Image".

3.For a chart, set it up with the usual chart controls (node, parameter, frequency, chart type — see Forecast results: Charts), then click "Add this chart to dashboard" ("Add charts to dashboard" in Multi-Chart mode).

4.While editing, drag the tiles to move or resize them, or delete them. Double-click a title to edit its text and font.

5.Click "Save". Precept asks "Share this dashboard with the whole team?" — Yes makes it visible to everyone on the asset, No keeps it private to you.

2.2 Open or change a dashboard

•"Load dashboard" lists your dashboards and the shared ones. Select one and click "Load", or "Set as default" to open it automatically next time.

•"Edit" switches an open dashboard to editing ("Add elements" and "Save" appear).

3. Generating a report

"Generate report" turns a saved dashboard into a Word (.docx) document. No Word installation is needed. You need at least one saved dashboard for the asset; otherwise Precept says so and stops.

1.Click "Generate report" in the "Forecast Results" group.

2.Choose the "Dashboard" and the "Scenario".

3.Under "Output", choose either: "New Word document (append each dashboard tile in order)" — a title block, then each tile with its title as a heading; or "Fill an existing Word document (replace {{Tile title}} placeholders)" — click "Browse..." and pick your own template.

4.Click "Generate" and choose where to save the report.

Template rules:

•Write {{Oil production}} where the tile titled "Oil production" must go. It is replaced by the chart or table image; your own headings and text stay as they are.

•Write {{ai: summarise the constraints of the forecast}} to have Precept AI write a paragraph there. The AI only receives the headline numbers of the dashboard, the constraint and shut-in events of the forecast, and the data of a chart you name in square brackets (e.g. {{ai: comment on [Oil production]}}).

4. Gain per opportunity

This workflow measures what each opportunity adds: it runs the scenario once as it is (the "Baseline"), then once without each opportunity in turn. The gain of an opportunity is the difference between the baseline and the run without it.

1.Save the scenario, then click "Gain per opportunity" in the "Forecast" group.

2.In the "Opportunity Gain Workflow" panel, choose "Calculate Opportunity gain for:" — "All Opportunities" or one project — and the date in "Calculate for Opportunities executed before:" (the forecast end date by default).

3.Click "Calculate" and confirm. One forecast runs per opportunity; the progress bar shows "Forecast without <opportunity>". Rig moves and opportunities with no impact on the forecast are skipped.

4.Open "Charts" and choose "Opportunity Gain" in the mode list to see the gains (see Forecast results: Charts).

The scenario itself is not modified: each "without" run uses a temporary copy. You can stop the workflow with the progress bar's stop button; the opportunities already calculated are kept.
