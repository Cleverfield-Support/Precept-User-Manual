Platform features shared by Forecast and Monitoring

 

1. Overview

Precept has two modules: Forecast (scenarios, well potentials, sequences, constraints and the forecast itself) and Monitoring (day-to-day surveillance of the producing and injecting wells). Around them, a set of platform features is shared by both modules. They are described in this book:

•Precept AI — the built-in assistant. It changes forecast inputs from a plain-English request, explains forecast results, answers how-to questions from this manual, and builds charts from questions about your production data.

•3D visualiser — a viewer for reservoir simulation grids and their results, with the production network on top.

•Petex GAP / Prosper and ECLIPSE links — exchange with Petroleum Experts GAP and Prosper, and with reservoir simulation models.

•Action Tracker — the asset's shared log of actions, with owners, due dates and threaded updates.

•Data sources and asset configuration — the data Precept reads, how to load your own data, and what your administrator configures.

2. Where to find them

Feature

Where

Precept AI

"Precept AI" on the main ribbon (in the group at the right-hand end, next to "Security"). In the Field Monitoring window: "Precept AI" and "My Custom charts".

3D visualiser

"3D model visualization" in the "3D Modelling" group of the main ribbon. "3D Model Visualiser" in the Field Monitoring window.

GAP / Prosper / ECLIPSE

The "GAP" and "3D Modelling" groups of the main ribbon. They are shown only when the matching option is ticked in Settings, under "Pre / Post Processing".

Action Tracker

"Actions Tracker" in the Field Monitoring window. Also the "Ongoing action" part of a well's status card in the producer and injector monitoring windows.

Data and configuration

"Asset Model" and "Import Asset data" in the Field Monitoring window. The "Unit" setting in Settings.

The Field Monitoring window opens from the "Asset" button, in the "Monitoring" group of the main ribbon. That group is shown when the Monitoring module is enabled for your asset.

3. Help and information

Two more buttons sit in the same group as "Precept AI" on the main ribbon:

•"User Manual" opens this manual in your web browser.

•"About Precept" shows the installed version ("Version: …"), the support e-mail address, phone number and website, and a "What's new?" button that opens the release notes.
