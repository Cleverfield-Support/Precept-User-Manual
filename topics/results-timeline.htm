Forecast results: Timeline

 

1. What the timeline shows

The timeline, in the main window, is the fastest way to QC a forecast. After a run, it shows for every node and every well, at every date, what the solver did: which nodes were the bottlenecks, which wells were shut in or choked back, when shutdowns, new wells and opportunities took effect.

•The top part shows the sequences (rigs, maintenance, construction…) with their opportunities.

•Below come the network nodes, then the wells, one row each.

•Hover over a cell to read its status (for example the constrained stream of a node, or why a well is shut in).

2. Colour code

2.1 Nodes — bottlenecks

A coloured node cell means that the node was at one of its capacities at that date, so it limited the forecast:

Colour

Node status

Green

Oil constrained

Light blue

Water constrained

Red

Gas constrained

Orange

Gas-lift constrained

Purple

Liquid constrained

Navy

Water injection constrained

Dark red

Gas injection constrained

Brown (chocolate)

Pressure constrained

Black

Capacity of 0 (the node is closed, e.g. during a shutdown)

2.2 Wells

Colour

Well status

Green / navy / dark red

Producing / injecting water / injecting gas at full potential (shown when "Show Prod / Inj" is ticked; white otherwise).

Light green

Producer choked back by a process constraint.

Blue / dark red

Water injector / gas injector choked back by a process constraint (dark red is also used for a producer limited by a gas constraint).

Dark grey

Shut in by a process constraint, or because there is not enough drawdown to flow.

Black

Closed: shutdown or planned shutdown, dead well, or well shut in as not economical.

Light grey

Abandoned, no potential or no more profile; exploration and appraisal wells (they never produce in the forecast).

Purple

Incremental or replacement profile; well limited by its maximum drawdown or minimum flowing BHP; VLP message.

Red / pink

Warnings: red for crossflow or a negative pressure, pink for a convergence problem. Check these wells.

Other colours

Shutdown categories use the colour chosen for them in Settings. A conversion is shown in the colour of the new well type (green to oil producer, red to gas producer, navy to water injector, dark red to gas injector).

3. Timeline controls

•"Show Timeline" — shows or hides the timeline.

•"View all timeline" — zooms out to the whole forecast period.

•"Show Prod / Inj" — colours the cells of wells that flow normally (green, navy, dark red), not only the exceptions.

•"Filter abandoned Wells" — removes the abandoned wells from the list.

•The filter above the node list ("Contains" and a text box) keeps only the rows whose name contains the text you type.

4. QC checklist

•Find the bottlenecks of the network: which node is constrained, on which stream, and for how long.

•Check that the shutdowns are where and when they should be.

•Check that new wells and new profiles come online at the right dates, and that conversions happen.

•Look for wells in black or dark grey that you expected to produce, and for warnings (red, pink).

•For the pressures and rates behind a status, open the network view ("Show Network", see Network view) or the charts (see Forecast results: Charts).
