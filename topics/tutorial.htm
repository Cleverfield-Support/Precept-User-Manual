Tutorials

 

The tutorials walk you through a complete Precept workflow, step by step, with the exact buttons to click. Follow them in order the first time you use Precept.

1. Available tutorials

•Create new Exploration Asset and generate forecasts — create an asset, a project and a scenario from scratch, draw or adapt the network, add producers and injectors, give them decline-curve potentials, schedule their drilling with a rig, set the objective function, run the forecast and look at the results.

2. Where to go next

Once the tutorial is done, these pages take each step further:

•Drawing a network with the icon palette: Network editor.

•Forecasting from reservoir physics instead of decline curves: Material Balance.

•Reading the results: Forecast results: Timeline and Network view.

•Asking Precept how to do something, or asking it to make a change for you: Precept AI.

•Short demonstrations of other workflows: Videos, and answers to common questions: FAQ.
